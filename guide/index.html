Holiday Party Planning Checklist

December calendars fill fast, so the invitation goes out early and the rest follows.

4 to 6 weeks before

  • Pick the date and time, and a start and end time
  • Set the guest count and the budget
  • Choose the style: dinner, open house, cookie exchange, cocktail party or ugly sweater party
  • Send the invitations with an RSVP date

2 to 3 weeks before

  • Plan the menu and the drinks, and note any allergies from the RSVPs
  • Make a playlist
  • Buy or borrow extra glasses, plates and chairs
  • Plan one activity: a gift swap, a cookie table or a photo corner

The week of

  • Send a short reminder to everyone who said yes
  • Shop for food and drinks, and make anything that keeps
  • Set up the decorations, the lights and the coat space

The day of

  • Set out the food an hour before, and the ice last
  • Leave the door unlocked or the porch light on
  • Have containers ready so guests can take treats home

Baby Shower Planning Checklist, 6 Weeks Out

6 weeks before

  • Agree on the date, the place and the guest list with the parent-to-be
  • Set the budget and who is co-hosting
  • Choose a theme, if you want one

4 to 6 weeks before

  • Send the invitations with the registry details and an RSVP date
  • Book any rentals or a caterer

2 to 3 weeks before

  • Plan the menu, the cake and the drinks
  • Choose two or three games, and get the supplies
  • Order or make the decorations and any favors

The week of

  • Confirm the final count and any food needs
  • Set out a table and a pen for gifts and cards, so thank-you notes are easy later
  • Ask one person to keep a list of who gave what

Birthday Party Planning Checklist for Grown-Ups

  • 3 to 4 weeks before: pick the date, the place and the guest list
  • 2 to 4 weeks before: send the invitations with an RSVP date
  • 2 weeks before: book a table or plan the food and drinks at home
  • 1 week before: order the cake, and plan a toast or one small surprise
  • The week of: send a reminder with the address and the parking details
  • The day of: candles, a lighter and a knife for the cake

Going Away Party Ideas

Easy ways to send someone off well:

  • Theme the food after where they are going
  • Pass around a card or a notebook for everyone to write in
  • Make a map with pins for where friends will visit them
  • Collect photos into a small printed album
  • Plan a short toast from two or three friends
  • Give a group gift they can pack, such as a travel journal
  • Keep it close to the moving date, but not the night before
  • Send the invitation two to three weeks ahead

What to Include on a Party Invitation

The seven details guests look for:

  1. What the party is, and who it is for
  2. The date and the day of the week
  3. The start time, and an end time if there is one
  4. The full address, with parking or a door code
  5. Who is hosting
  6. How to RSVP, and by when
  7. Anything to bring or wear: a dish, a gift for a swap, an ugly sweater

Read it once as a guest would. If you would need to text the host a question, add the answer.

RSVP Wording: How to Ask Guests to Reply

Kindly reply by November 20 to Sam at 555-0123.
Please RSVP by text to Jordan by May 2.
Let us know you're coming by June 1. We'd love to save you a seat.
Regrets only to Maya by December 5.
Kindly reply by the first of September. Note any dietary needs.

"Regrets only" means guests reply only if they cannot come. Use it for a casual party, not when you need an exact count.

How to Edit an Invitation Template in Canva

  1. Open the template link from your download on a computer, phone or tablet.
  2. Sign in to Canva or create a free account.
  3. Select Use template. This makes your own copy, and the original stays as it was.
  4. Click any text to change it: names, the date, the time and the address.
  5. Change the fonts, the colors and the text size from the top bar, if you like.
  6. Add a photo from Uploads, if the design has room for one.
  7. Select Share, then Download. Choose PDF Print to print, or PNG to send by text or email.

A computer is easiest for small text. Zoom in to check each line before you download.

What Size to Print a 5x7 Invitation

  • Size: 5 x 7 inches, the most common invitation size.
  • Envelope: an A7 envelope, 5 1/4 x 7 1/4 inches.
  • Paper: card stock. A heavier cover weight feels sturdier, and a matte finish prints most evenly at home.
  • At home: download the PDF Print file, set the printer to actual size, and turn off "Fit to page". Print one test first.
  • At a print shop: bring the PDF Print file and ask for 5 x 7 on card stock. Ask whether they trim.
  • Colors: a screen and a printer show color differently, so a test print saves a whole stack.
