How to Print an Invitation at Home (5x7)

Size, paper and envelope

  • Size: 5 x 7 inches, a common invitation size.
  • Envelope: an A7 envelope, which measures 5 1/4 x 7 1/4 inches.
  • Paper: card stock. A heavier cover weight feels sturdier, and a matte finish prints evenly on most home printers.

Print at home, step by step

  1. In Canva, choose Share, then Download, and pick PDF Print.
  2. Open the PDF on your computer and open the print window.
  3. Set the scale to 100 percent or actual size, and turn off "Fit to page" so the card does not shrink.
  4. Choose your card stock under paper type if your printer offers it, and pick the highest quality setting.
  5. Print one test on plain paper and hold it against a ruler to check that it measures 5 x 7 inches.
  6. Print on card stock, and let the ink dry before you stack or fold anything.

A 5 x 7 card is smaller than a letter sheet. Print it on a full sheet and trim it to size, or load 5 x 7 paper if your printer accepts it. If the download dialog offers crop marks and bleed, turn them on when you plan to trim.

Use a print shop

  • Bring the PDF Print file and ask for 5 x 7 on card stock.
  • Ask whether they trim the cards for you.
  • Ask for one proof before you order the full count.

About color

A screen and a printer show color differently, so a test print saves a whole stack of card stock.
