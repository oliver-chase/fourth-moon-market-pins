Fitting cards on a letter sheet

A letter sheet measures 8 1/2 x 11 inches. One 5 x 7 card fits on a sheet with margins, and four 4 1/4 x 5 1/2 cards fit on one sheet. Print, then trim.

Tips

  • Match the envelope to the card size before you print.
  • A square card or an odd shape may need extra postage.
  • Print one test on plain paper and measure it.

Common questions

What size is a standard invitation?

A 5 x 7 inch card is a common size, with a 4 x 6 inch card as a smaller option.

Which envelope fits a 5x7 invitation?

An A7 envelope, which measures 5 1/4 x 7 1/4 inches.
