Holiday Open House Invitation Wording

Wording for a holiday open house invitation, including the drop-in window, the food, and what to say about kids and parking.

An open house lets guests arrive any time inside a window. The invitation has one extra job: it must say that guests can come and go, and it must give the window.

State the drop-in window

Write the hours as a range, such as 2:00 to 6:00 pm, and add a line such as Drop in any time. A window of three to four hours gives guests room to stop by between other plans.

Open house wording

Open house. Come by for cocoa, cookies and good company. Saturday, December 14, anytime from 2:00 to 6:00 pm, at 12 Maple Lane.
Our door is open. Join us for a holiday open house on Sunday, December 15, from 1:00 to 5:00 pm. Light bites and warm drinks. No need to reply, just come.
Stop in for a little holiday cheer. Friday, December 20, 5:00 to 8:00 pm. Neighbors, friends and family welcome. Please let Jordan know if you can make it by December 13.

The names, dates and addresses in these examples are placeholders. Replace each one with your own details.

Choices to make before you write

  • Reply or no reply: for a pure drop-in, you can say no reply needed. For food that needs a count, ask for a reply by a set date.
  • Food: say whether you are serving a meal, appetizers or sweets, so guests know whether to eat first.
  • Kids and pets: say whether children are welcome and whether pets are in the house.
  • Shoes and coats: tell guests where to leave them if winter weather is likely.

When to send

Send an open house invitation 4 to 6 weeks ahead, the same as other holiday parties. See when to send Christmas party invitations for dates.

Common questions

What is the difference between an open house and a party?

An open house runs for a window of hours and guests arrive and leave as they like. A party has a start time and most guests arrive together.

Do I need guests to RSVP to an open house?

A reply is optional when you serve simple food. Ask for one if you need a count for a meal or a limited space.
