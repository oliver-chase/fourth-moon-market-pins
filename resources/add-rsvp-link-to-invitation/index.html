How to Add an RSVP Link to a Canva Invitation

Collect replies online for free: make an RSVP form, add its link to a texted invitation and a QR code to a printed one.

Many paid invitation sites include online replies. You can get the same result with a printable template and a free form. Make the form once, then put its link on the invitation you text and a QR code on the one you print.

1. Make a free RSVP form

  1. Open a free form tool you already use, such as Google Forms or Microsoft Forms
  2. Add three questions: your name, will you attend (yes or no), and how many guests
  3. Add any extra question you need, such as dietary needs or which dish you will bring
  4. Turn on email or phone notifications so you see each reply
  5. Copy the share link, and shorten it if the tool offers a short link

Send yourself a test reply before the invitation goes out, so you know the form works.

2. Add the link to an invitation you text or email

  1. Open your copy of the template in Canva
  2. Add a line such as: Reply by December 5 at the link below
  3. Download the card as a PNG or PDF
  4. Send the image, then paste the form link in the same message, since a link inside an image cannot be tapped

3. Add a QR code to a printed invitation

  1. In Canva, open Apps and search for QR code
  2. Paste your form link and add the code to the card
  3. Make the code at least 0.75 inch wide and keep a white margin around it
  4. Add a short line beside it, such as: Scan to RSVP by December 5
  5. Print one test copy and scan it with your phone before you print the rest

RSVP lines to copy

Kindly reply by December 5 at the link below.
Scan the code to RSVP by May 2.
Let us know you are coming by June 1. The link takes 30 seconds.
Regrets only, by December 11, at the link or by text to Sam.

The names, dates and addresses in these examples are placeholders. Replace each one with your own details.

Common questions

Can guests reply without an account?

Yes, if you set the form so it does not require a sign-in. Check this setting before you share the link.

Should I still list a phone number?

Add one for guests who prefer to text. A short line such as: or text Sam at 555-0123 covers it.
