Ugly Sweater Party Invitation Wording and Rules

An ugly sweater party invitation needs one clear instruction: wear the sweater. Add a prize and a start time, and the rest writes itself.

Wording examples

Ugly sweater party. The worst sweater wins a prize. Saturday, December 14, doors open at 7:00 pm. Reply by December 7.
Dust off the itchiest sweater you own. Prizes for the ugliest, the most festive and the best light-up. Friday, December 13, 7:00 pm, at 12 Maple Lane.
Tacky is the dress code. Come in your best ugly Christmas sweater for food, a contest and a photo wall. Sunday, December 15, from 4:00 pm.

The names, dates and addresses in these examples are placeholders. Replace each one with your own details.

Prize categories

  • Ugliest sweater
  • Most festive
  • Best homemade
  • Best light-up or musical sweater
  • Best group costume

Rules to put on the card

  • Wear an ugly sweater to enter the contest.
  • Thrifted, borrowed and homemade sweaters all count.
  • Voting happens at a set time, such as 8:00 pm.

Setup for the contest

  1. Pick a judge or let guests vote by paper slip.
  2. Set up a photo corner with good light.
  3. Announce winners and hand out the prizes.

Timing

Send the invitation 4 to 6 weeks ahead. See when to send Christmas party invitations.

Common questions

Should I give guests a prize?

A prize adds fun and costs little. Small gifts, a trophy or a bottle of something festive all work.

What if a guest has no sweater?

Say that thrifted and borrowed sweaters count, and add a line that a festive shirt or a sweater vest is welcome.
