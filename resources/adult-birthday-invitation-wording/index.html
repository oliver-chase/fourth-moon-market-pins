An adult birthday invitation can be warm, funny or formal. The examples below cover the common styles.

Dinner and cake

Celebrate with us. Dana turns 40. Friday, September 12, 7:00 pm, dinner and cake at Pasta Lane. Reply by September 5.
Dinner is on us. Join us for Dana's 40th. Friday, September 12, 7:00 pm. Reply to Sam by September 5.

Open house

Come for the cake, stay for the stories. Open house birthday, drop in any time from 2:00 to 6:00 pm.

Milestone birthdays

Dana is turning 50. Help us celebrate. Saturday, September 13, at 6:00 pm.
Thirty, flirty and thriving. Come celebrate Dana's 30th.

Surprise party

Shh. It's a surprise. Dana turns 40. Be there by 6:30 pm, and park on the next street. Reply to Sam only, by September 5.

For a surprise, ask guests to reply to the host and not to the guest of honor, and give an arrival time ahead of the guest of honor.

Gift lines

  • No gifts, please.
  • Your company is the gift.
  • Bring a favorite memory to share.

The names, dates and addresses in these examples are placeholders. Replace each one with your own details.

Common questions

What should an adult birthday invitation say?

The guest of honor, the date, the time, the place, the reply details and a line about gifts.

How do I keep a surprise party secret?

Ask guests to reply to the host, give an arrival time before the guest of honor, and avoid group chats that include them.
