Resources › Save the date

A save the date is short. It gives the names, the date and the place, and it tells guests that the invitation will come later.

What to include

  1. The names of the couple
  2. The date
  3. The city or the venue
  4. A note that a formal invitation will follow
  5. A wedding website, if you have one

Wording examples

Save the date. Alex and Jamie are getting married on June 6. Invitation to follow.
We're getting married. Mark June 6 on your calendar. Details and a website to come.
Save the date for the wedding of Alex Taylor and Jamie Chen. June 6, Denver, Colorado. Formal invitation to follow.
Hold the date. June 6 in the mountains. Alex and Jamie are tying the knot.

The names, dates and addresses in these examples are placeholders. Replace each one with your own details.

Tips

  • Leave out the full address, the dress code and the reply date. The invitation holds those.
  • Give a travel note for a destination wedding, such as the city and a hotel block.
  • Match the design of the save the date to the invitation.

Common questions

What should a save the date say?

The names, the date, the place and a note that the invitation will follow.

Do I need to include a website?

A website is useful when you have one with hotel and travel details. A save the date works without one.
