A baby shower invitation names the guest of honor, gives the date and place, and says how to find the registry. These examples cover the common tones.

What to include

  1. The name of the guest of honor
  2. The host's name
  3. The date, the time and the address
  4. A theme, if there is one
  5. Registry details
  6. The reply date and how to reply

Wording examples

Join us for a baby shower honoring Priya. Sunday, April 20, 2:00 pm, at 12 Maple Lane. Lunch, games and a little time to celebrate. Hosted by Maya. Kindly reply by April 6.
A little one is on the way. Come shower Priya and Sam with love. Registry details are on the back.
Diapers and wipes are welcome. Come celebrate with Priya and Sam at a baby sprinkle on April 20, 2:00 pm.
Co-ed shower. Come eat, play games and welcome the baby with Priya and Sam. April 20, 2:00 pm.

The names, dates and addresses in these examples are placeholders. Replace each one with your own details.

Registry lines

  • Registry details are on the back.
  • Priya and Sam are registered at the store named on the card.
  • No gifts are required. Your company means the most.

Common questions

What should a baby shower invitation say?

The guest of honor, the host, the date, time and address, the registry details and a reply date.

Is it okay to put registry information on the invitation?

Many hosts do. Put it on the back or on a small insert so the front stays about the celebration.
