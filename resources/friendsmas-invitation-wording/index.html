Friendsmas Invitation Wording and Ideas

Friendsmas invitation wording to copy: a holiday get-together for friends, with lines for a potluck, a gift swap and a casual hang.

Friendsmas is a holiday gathering with friends. The invitation can be as casual as a text, and the wording below sets the tone: warm, a little funny and clear about what to bring.

Wording examples

You're invited to Friendsmas. Our favorite holiday, with our favorite people. Saturday, December 12, 6:00 pm. Bring a dish and a drink to share.
Friendsmas is on. Food, a gift swap with a $20 limit, and the good kind of chaos. Sunday, December 13, 5:00 pm. Reply by December 6.
The family holidays are coming. Before they do, come celebrate with us. Friendsmas, Friday, December 11, 7:00 pm, at our place.

The names, dates and addresses in these examples are placeholders. Replace each one with your own details.

What to put on the card

  • The name of the party: Friendsmas, Friendsgiving-style potluck or holiday friends night
  • The format: potluck, dinner, gift swap or movie night
  • A price limit for any gift exchange
  • The reply date
  • A dress code, if there is one, such as pajamas or ugly sweaters

Pairing the invitation

Use the same design on a text invitation, an event page and a printed card, so guests recognize it. See how to host a Friendsmas for the plan.

Common questions

What is Friendsmas?

It is a holiday celebration with friends. Hosts choose the format, such as a potluck, a dinner or a gift swap.

When should I send Friendsmas invitations?

Send them 4 to 6 weeks before, since December schedules fill early.
