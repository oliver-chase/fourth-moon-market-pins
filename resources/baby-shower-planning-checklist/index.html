Baby Shower Planning Checklist

A week-by-week baby shower planning checklist covering the guest list, invitations, food, games, gifts and thank-you notes.

A baby shower comes together in four stages: decide, invite, prepare and host. This checklist counts back from the day.

6 to 8 weeks before

  • Agree on the date, the place and the guest list with the parent-to-be
  • Set the budget and decide who is co-hosting
  • Choose a theme, if you want one

4 to 6 weeks before

  • Send the invitations with the registry details and a reply date
  • Book any rentals or a caterer

2 to 3 weeks before

  • Plan the menu, the cake and the drinks
  • Choose two or three games and get the supplies
  • Order or make the decorations and any favors

The week of

  • Confirm the final count and any food needs
  • Set out a table and a pen for gifts and cards, so thank-you notes are easy later
  • Ask one person to keep a list of who gave what

The day of

  • Set up the food table and a gift table
  • Place a guest book and a pen at the entrance
  • Take photos of the gift opening for the parent-to-be

After the shower

  • Send thank-you notes within a few weeks
  • Return rental items
  • Share photos with the host and guests

Common questions

What do I need for a baby shower?

A date, a place, a guest list, invitations, food, a few games, a gift table and a way to track who gave what.

How long should a baby shower last?

Two to three hours is common.
