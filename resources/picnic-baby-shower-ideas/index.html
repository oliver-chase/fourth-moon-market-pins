A picnic or farmers market shower is relaxed and seasonal. The invitation sets the scene and the plan covers the weather.

Invitation wording

A picnic for a little one. Join us in the park for a baby shower honoring Priya and Sam. Saturday, May 10, 12:00 pm. Bring a blanket or a chair.
Locally grown and well loved. Come to a farmers market baby shower for Priya and Sam. Saturday, September 20, 11:00 am, at 12 Maple Lane. Brunch and fresh fruit.

The names, dates and addresses in these examples are placeholders. Replace each one with your own details.

Setup

  • Quilts and blankets for the ground and a few chairs for guests who prefer them
  • Baskets, crates and jars for the food
  • Lemonade, iced tea and sparkling water
  • Seasonal fruit, sandwiches and a simple cake
  • A shaded area and a table for gifts

Plan for weather and comfort

  • Choose a park shelter or a covered patio as a rain plan, and write the backup on the invitation.
  • Check for restrooms and parking at the site.
  • Reserve the shelter or pavilion if the park requires it.
  • Bring bug spray, sunscreen and water.
  • Offer a seat in the shade for the guest of honor.

Common questions

What should I serve at a picnic baby shower?

Sandwiches, fresh fruit, salads, a simple cake and drinks in a cooler or a jug.

What if it rains?

Name a backup on the invitation, such as a covered patio or a living room, so guests know where to go.
