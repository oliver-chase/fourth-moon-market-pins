When to Send Wedding Invitations: A Timeline

Send wedding invitations 6 to 8 weeks before the wedding, or 10 to 12 weeks for a destination wedding, with the RSVP due 3 to 4 weeks out.

Most wedding invitations go out 6 to 8 weeks before the day. Destination weddings need longer, since guests book travel and time off.

Timeline

PieceStandard weddingDestination wedding
Save the date6 to 8 months before8 to 12 months before
Invitation6 to 8 weeks before10 to 12 weeks before
RSVP due3 to 4 weeks beforeAs the venue and caterer ask

Zola, Greenvelope and The Knot give the same ranges. Treat them as rules of thumb and ask your venue and caterer for their own deadlines.

Count back from the wedding date

For a wedding on June 6, 8 weeks before is April 11 and 6 weeks before is April 25. Send the invitations between those two dates. A reply date of May 9 to May 16 gives the caterer a count 3 to 4 weeks ahead.

Build in time to address and mail

  • Order or print invitations 2 to 3 weeks before the mailing date
  • Address the envelopes in the week before mailing
  • Weigh a complete invitation at the post office so the postage is right
  • Mail all invitations on one day

After you send

  • Follow up with guests who have not replied after the RSVP date.
  • Give the final count to the caterer by the date they set.
  • Send a short reminder with the schedule in the last two weeks.

Common questions

How far in advance should wedding invitations go out?

Send them 6 to 8 weeks before the wedding. Destination weddings call for 10 to 12 weeks.

What RSVP date should I choose?

Pick a date 3 to 4 weeks before the wedding so you can give the caterer a head count and make a seating plan.

Do I need to send a save the date too?

Most couples do. See when to send save the dates.
