When to Send

When invitations go out and when to ask for replies, by event.

Timeline by event

EventSend the invitationAsk for an RSVP by
Wedding6 to 8 weeks before3 to 4 weeks before
Save the date6 to 8 months beforeNo RSVP
Baby shower4 to 6 weeks before1 to 2 weeks before
Holiday party4 to 6 weeks before1 to 2 weeks before
Birthday party2 to 4 weeks before1 week before
Going away party2 to 3 weeks before1 week before

These are common rules of thumb. Send earlier for a busy season or for guests who travel.

Reminders

  • Send a short reminder to everyone who said yes in the week of the event.
  • Follow up once with guests who have not replied after the RSVP date, since a guest count helps with food and seating.
  • Give the RSVP date as a calendar date, so no guest has to work it out.
