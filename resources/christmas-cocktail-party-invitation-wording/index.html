Christmas Cocktail Party Invitation Wording

Christmas cocktail party invitation wording with dress code lines, start and end times, and notes on food and non-alcoholic drinks.

A cocktail party invitation sets a dressier tone and a shorter window. Give the hours, name the dress code and tell guests what food to expect.

Wording examples

Join us for holiday cocktails. Friday, December 13, 6:30 to 9:30 pm, at 12 Maple Lane. Cocktail attire. Heavy appetizers will be served. Kindly reply by December 6.
Sip, snack and celebrate the season. Saturday, December 14, 7:00 to 10:00 pm. Festive attire. Reply to Jordan by December 7.
An evening of cocktails, canapes and carols. Sunday, December 15, 5:00 to 8:00 pm. We would love to see you.

The names, dates and addresses in these examples are placeholders. Replace each one with your own details.

Dress code lines

  • Cocktail attire
  • Festive attire
  • Holiday glam
  • Dress up, but wear shoes you can stand in

Drinks and food

  • Say whether the evening has dinner or appetizers only.
  • Offer at least one non-alcoholic drink and mention it.
  • List a signature drink on the invitation, if you have one.

Timing

Send it 4 to 6 weeks ahead. See when to send Christmas party invitations.

Common questions

What time should a cocktail party start and end?

Three hours is a common length. A start between 5:00 and 7:00 pm lets guests come from work and still make plans afterward.

Do I need to say what food is served?

Yes. Guests decide whether to eat first when the card says appetizers only.
