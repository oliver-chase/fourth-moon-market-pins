A Christmas party invitation works best when it sets the mood in one line and then gives the facts. Below are examples in four tones, then the details that save you from answering the same text ten times.

What every Christmas party invitation needs

  1. The kind of party: dinner, open house, cocktail party, cookie exchange or gift swap
  2. The date, with the day of the week
  3. A start time and an end time, so guests can plan sitters and travel
  4. The full address, with parking or a door code
  5. Who is hosting
  6. How to RSVP, and the date to reply by
  7. Anything to bring or wear, such as a dish or a sweater

The names, dates and addresses in these examples are placeholders. Replace each one with your own details.

Classic wording

Join us for an evening of holiday cheer. Saturday, December 12, 6:00 to 9:00 pm, at the Rivera home, 12 Maple Lane. Dinner and drinks will be served. Kindly reply by December 5 to Sam at 555-0123.
You are warmly invited to celebrate the season with us. Friday, December 18, at 7:00 in the evening. Light supper and dessert. Regrets only to Maya by December 11.

Casual wording

Come eat, drink and be merry. Saturday, December 12, 6:00 pm. Bring a dish to share and we will cover the drinks. Text Jordan by December 7 if you are coming.
The holidays are better with you in the room. Cocoa, cookies and bad holiday movies at our place, December 19, 4:00 pm.

Playful wording

Eat. Drink. Argue about the playlist. Our annual Christmas party is back, Saturday, December 12, from 6:00 pm until the last guest leaves.
Santa is busy, so we are throwing the party ourselves. December 18, 7:00 pm. Reply by December 11 so we know how many cookies to hide.

Extra lines worth adding

  • Dress code: festive, cocktail attire or ugly sweaters
  • Food: dinner served, appetizers only, or potluck
  • Kids: say whether children are welcome, with the same words on every invitation
  • Plus ones: name the guests who are invited on the envelope or in the message
  • Gifts: a gift swap needs a price limit written on the invitation
  • Parking: where guests can park and whether the street needs a permit

Read it once as a guest

  • Date and day of the week match
  • Start and end times are both present
  • The address is complete
  • The reply date and the way to reply are clear
  • Nothing runs off the edge of the page when you download it

Common questions

Should a Christmas party invitation have an end time?

An end time helps guests plan sitters, rides and the next day. A line such as 6:00 to 9:00 pm gives that, and an open house can use a drop-in window.

What should I write if I want no gifts?

Add a short line such as Your company is the gift. If you plan a gift swap, write the price limit on the invitation instead.

How do I ask guests to bring food?

State the kind of dish you want, such as a dessert or a side, and say whether you will provide the main course. That keeps the table balanced.
