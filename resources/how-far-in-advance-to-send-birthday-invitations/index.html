How Far in Advance to Send Birthday Invitations

Send adult birthday invitations 2 to 4 weeks ahead and ask for replies about a week before, with extra time for milestones and travel.

An adult birthday party invitation goes out 2 to 4 weeks ahead. Send earlier for a big milestone or when guests travel.

Timeline

StepWhen
Send the invitation2 to 4 weeks before
Ask for a reply byAbout 1 week before
Send a reminderIn the week of the party

Evite gives 2 to 4 weeks for adult birthdays. It is a rule of thumb.

Send earlier when

  • The party is a milestone such as 30, 40 or 50
  • Guests live out of town
  • The date is near a holiday
  • The venue needs a booking

For a surprise party

Ask guests to reply to the host, and send a short reminder with the arrival time ahead of the guest of honor.
