Resources › Going away party

A going away party invitation says who is leaving, where they are going and when to say goodbye. A light tone helps.

Wording examples

Chris is moving to Denver. Come say goodbye over tacos on Saturday, August 9, at 6:00 pm. Bring a favorite memory to share.
One last night together. Join us to send Lena off. Reply by Friday to help us plan the food.
We'll miss you. Farewell party for Chris on Friday, August 8, at 5:00 pm.

The names, dates and addresses in these examples are placeholders. Replace each one with your own details.

Wording for a coworker

Join us in the break room to wish Lena well. Thursday, August 7, at 3:00 pm. Cake and a card to sign.

Details to include

  • Who is leaving and where they are going
  • The date and the time
  • The address
  • How to reply
  • What to bring: a memory, a photo or a card

What should a going away invitation say?

Going away or farewell?

Both are in common use. Pick the one your friends say.
