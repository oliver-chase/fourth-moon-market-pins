Resources › Going away party

A going away party takes one evening and a little planning. Set the date early and pick a few ways to make it personal.

Timeline

2 to 4 weeks before

  • Pick the date, close to the moving date but not the night before
  • Send the invitation with a reply date

1 to 2 weeks before

  • Plan the food, the drinks and a group gift
  • Ask two or three friends to prepare a toast

The week of

  • Send a reminder with the address
  • Buy a card or a notebook for everyone to write in

The day of

  • Set out the food and the card
  • Plan time for the toast
  • Take a group photo

Ideas

  • Theme the food after where they are going
  • Pass around a card or a notebook
  • Make a map with pins for where friends will visit
  • Collect photos into a small printed album
  • Give a group gift they can pack, such as a travel journal

Common questions

How far in advance should I plan a going away party?

Send the invitation 2 to 4 weeks ahead.

What is a good group gift?

Something small that packs well, such as a travel journal, a framed photo or a card with notes from everyone.
