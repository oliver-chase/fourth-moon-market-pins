Holiday party

4 to 6 weeks before

  • Pick the date, a start time and an end time
  • Set the guest count and the budget
  • Choose the style: dinner, open house, cookie exchange, cocktail party or ugly sweater party
  • Send the invitations with an RSVP date

2 to 3 weeks before

  • Plan the menu and the drinks, and note any allergies from the RSVPs
  • Make a playlist
  • Buy or borrow extra glasses, plates and chairs
  • Plan one activity: a gift swap, a cookie table or a photo corner

The week of

  • Send a short reminder to everyone who said yes
  • Shop for food and drinks, and make anything that keeps
  • Set up the decorations, the lights and the coat space

The day of

  • Set out the food an hour before, and the ice last
  • Leave the door unlocked or the porch light on
  • Have containers ready so guests can take treats home

Baby shower

6 weeks before

  • Agree on the date, the place and the guest list with the parent-to-be
  • Set the budget and decide who is co-hosting
  • Choose a theme, if you want one

4 to 6 weeks before

  • Send the invitations with the registry details and an RSVP date
  • Book any rentals or a caterer

2 to 3 weeks before

  • Plan the menu, the cake and the drinks
  • Choose two or three games and get the supplies
  • Order or make the decorations and any favors

The week of

  • Confirm the final count and any food needs
  • Set out a table and a pen for gifts and cards, so thank-you notes are easy later
  • Ask one person to keep a list of who gave what

Adult birthday party

  • 3 to 4 weeks before: pick the date, the place and the guest list
  • 2 to 4 weeks before: send the invitations with an RSVP date
  • 2 weeks before: book a table, or plan the food and drinks at home
  • 1 week before: order the cake, and plan a toast or one small surprise
  • The week of: send a reminder with the address and the parking details
  • The day of: candles, a lighter and a knife for the cake

Wedding paper goods

PieceWhen it goes out
Save the date6 to 8 months before, or 9 to 12 months for a destination wedding
Invitation6 to 8 weeks before
RSVP cardWith the invitation, due 3 to 4 weeks before the day
Details cardWith the invitation: hotels, directions, the website
Program, menu and signsPrinted in the last two weeks
Thank-you cardsWithin three months after the wedding

These are common rules of thumb. Ask your venue and planner about your own dates.

Going away party ideas

  • Theme the food after where they are going
  • Pass around a card or a notebook for everyone to write in
  • Make a map with pins for where friends will visit them
  • Collect photos into a small printed album
  • Plan a short toast from two or three friends
  • Give a group gift they can pack, such as a travel journal
  • Hold it close to the moving date and not the night before
  • Send the invitation two to four weeks ahead

Common questions

When do thank-you cards go out after a wedding?

Within three months after the wedding.
