What every invitation needs

  1. What the party is, and who it is for
  2. The date and the day of the week
  3. The start time, and an end time if there is one
  4. The full address, with parking or a door code
  5. Who is hosting
  6. How to RSVP, and by when
  7. Anything to bring or wear

Read it once as a guest would. If you would need to text the host a question, add the answer.

RSVP wording

Kindly reply by November 20 to Sam at 555-0123.
Please RSVP by text to Jordan by May 2.
Let us know you're coming by June 1. We'd love to save you a seat.
Regrets only to Maya by December 5.
Kindly reply by the first of September. Note any dietary needs.

"Regrets only" means guests reply only if they cannot come. Use it for a casual party, and use a plain "RSVP by" line when you need an exact count. The names and numbers above are placeholders for you to replace.

Holiday party wording

Join us for holiday cheer. Saturday, December 14, 6:00 to 9:00 pm, at the Rivera home, 12 Maple Lane. Food, drinks and good company. RSVP by December 7.
You're invited to our annual cookie exchange. Bring two dozen cookies and a container to take treats home.
Ugly sweater party. The worst sweater wins a prize. Doors open at 7:00 pm.

Wedding wording

Together with their families, Alex and Jamie invite you to celebrate their wedding. Saturday, June 6, at 4:00 in the afternoon. Reception to follow.
Alex and Jamie are getting married. Please join us for the ceremony and a dinner afterward. Kindly reply by May 1.

Save the date

Save the date. Alex and Jamie are getting married on June 6. Invitation to follow.
We're getting married. Mark June 6 on your calendar. Details and a website to come.

Baby shower wording

Join us for a baby shower honoring Priya. Sunday, April 20, 2:00 pm. Lunch, games and a little time to celebrate.
A little one is on the way. Come shower Priya and Sam with love. Registry details are on the back.

Birthday wording

Celebrate with us. Dana turns 40. Friday, September 12, 7:00 pm, dinner and cake at Pasta Lane. Reply by September 5.
Come for the cake, stay for the stories. Open house birthday, drop in any time from 2:00 to 6:00 pm.

Going away party wording

Chris is moving to Denver. Come say goodbye over tacos on Saturday, August 9, at 6:00 pm. Bring a favorite memory to share.
One last night together. Join us to send Lena off. Reply by Friday to help us plan the food.

All names, dates and addresses here are placeholders. Replace each one with your own details.
