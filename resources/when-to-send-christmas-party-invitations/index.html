When to Send Christmas Party Invitations

Send Christmas party invitations 4 to 6 weeks ahead and ask for replies 1 to 2 weeks before. See send dates for Saturdays in December 2026.

December calendars fill early, so a holiday party invitation goes out earlier than a regular party invitation. Plan on 4 to 6 weeks before the party, with the reply date 1 to 2 weeks before.

The timing rule

StepWhen
Send the invitation4 to 6 weeks before the party
Ask for replies by1 to 2 weeks before the party
Send a reminderIn the week of the party, to everyone who said yes
Follow up onceWith guests who have not replied after the reply date

These are common rules of thumb that several invitation and party-planning guides share. Ask your own guests about travel plans if many of them leave town for the holidays.

Each row counts back from the party date: 6 weeks for the earliest send date, 4 weeks for the latest, and 2 weeks and 1 week for the reply window.

If you are already inside four weeks

  • Send the invitation now by text or email and write the reply date in the first line.
  • Shorten the reply window to one week so you still get a head count.
  • Message the guests you most want to see before you send the group invitation, so they hold the date.
  • Use a drop-in format for an open house, since a window of hours is easier for a busy guest to say yes to.

Common questions

How far in advance should I send Christmas party invitations?

Send them 4 to 6 weeks before the party. For a party in the second week of December, that is late October to mid November.

When should the RSVP date be?

Set it 1 to 2 weeks before the party, which leaves time to plan food and seating.

Is it too early to send a holiday invitation in October?

A party in early December can go out in late October. Guests tend to answer sooner when the date is written on the card and they can add it to their calendar at once.
