How to Host a Cookie Exchange

A plain guide to hosting a cookie exchange: the cookie math, the setup, the allergy labels, and a timeline from invitation to cleanup.

A cookie exchange is a party where every guest bakes one kind of cookie and everyone leaves with a mixed box. The host sets the rules, sets out the table and keeps the cocoa warm.

The cookie math

If every guest brings the same number of cookies and everyone takes home the same number, each guest leaves with as many cookies as they brought, in many kinds. With ten guests who each bring two dozen, the table holds twenty dozen and each guest takes home two dozen. Ask guests to bring a few extra cookies for tasting at the party.

Timeline

4 to 6 weeks before

  • Pick the date and a 2 to 3 hour window
  • Choose the guest count your space can hold
  • Send the invitation with the rules and a reply date

2 weeks before

  • Confirm the head count
  • Tell guests the final number of cookies to bake
  • Collect allergy notes from the replies

The week of

  • Buy cocoa, coffee and napkins
  • Prepare labels and cards for each plate
  • Gather tins, boxes or bags for take-home

The day of

  • Clear a long table and cover it
  • Set out a plate or tray for each cookie type
  • Put a card by each plate with the cookie name, baker and allergens
  • Put out tongs and a small tray at each plate

How the party runs

  1. Guests arrive and set their cookies on the table.
  2. Everyone looks at the table and has a taste.
  3. Guests fill their containers, taking an equal number of each kind.
  4. Guests stay for cocoa, or head out with their box.

Small touches

  • A vote for best-looking and best-tasting cookie, with a small prize
  • A recipe card table, so guests copy the recipes they like
  • A donation plate for a local food bank, if you want to give back

Common questions

How many people should I invite?

Invite as many as your table and your living space can hold comfortably. A larger group brings more kinds of cookies and needs a bigger table.

What if someone cannot bake?

Let them buy cookies, bring a store-bought box or help with setup. Say so kindly in the invitation.

How do I keep cookies fresh?

Ask guests to bring cookies in a sealed container and keep them covered until the party starts.
