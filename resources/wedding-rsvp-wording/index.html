Wedding RSVP Wording for Cards and Websites

Wedding RSVP wording for a response card or a website, with meal choice lines, a reply deadline, and what to do when guests do not answer.

A response card has one job: a clear yes or no by a clear date. Keep the card short and say how to return it.

Response card wording

Kindly reply by May 9.
M ________________________ will attend / will not attend.
Number attending: ____
Please choose one entree: Chicken / Fish / Vegetarian.
Please tell us about any food allergies.

Wording for a wedding website

Kindly reply by May 9 at our wedding website.
Please RSVP online at the address on the details card. Let us know about any food allergies.

Choices to make

  • Put the name of each invited guest on the envelope or in the reply form, so only invited guests answer.
  • List meal choices only if the caterer needs them.
  • Add a pre-addressed, stamped envelope for mailed cards.
  • Add a reply date 3 to 4 weeks before the wedding.

When guests do not reply

  1. Wait a few days after the reply date.
  2. Send a short text or call: We need a head count by Friday. Can you make it?
  3. Record every answer in one list.
  4. Send the caterer the final count by their deadline.

The names, dates and addresses in these examples are placeholders. Replace each one with your own details.

Common questions

Should I put meal choices on the RSVP?

Only if the caterer needs them. Put each choice on the card with a space to mark it.

How do I handle plus ones?

Write the names of invited guests on the envelope or in the reply form, and state the number of seats reserved.
