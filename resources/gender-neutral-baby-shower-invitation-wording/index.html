A gender neutral invitation names the celebration without pronouns. The wording is simple, and the design carries the theme.

Wording examples

Baby on the way. Join us for a shower honoring Priya and Sam. Sunday, April 20, 2:00 pm. Lunch and games.
A new little one is coming. Come celebrate with Priya and Sam on April 20 at 2:00 pm.
Welcome, little one. A baby shower for Priya and Sam, hosted by Maya. April 20, 2:00 pm, at 12 Maple Lane.
Something sweet is on the way. Join us for a baby sprinkle on April 20.

The names, dates and addresses in these examples are placeholders. Replace each one with your own details.

Theme ideas

  • Wildflowers and greenery
  • A garden party
  • A picnic
  • A farmers market theme
  • Woodland animals
  • Stars and moons

Palette ideas

Greens, creams, oranges, yellows and soft earth tones suit a shower without a blue or pink code. Choose colors that fit the guest of honor's home and style.

Guest notes

  • Write a neutral line for gifts, such as Diapers and wipes are welcome.
  • Link to the registry or add it to a small insert.

Common questions

How do I word a baby shower invitation without naming the baby's sex?

Use baby, little one or new arrival, and put the guest of honor's name in the first line.

Do neutral showers need a neutral theme?

A theme such as a garden, a picnic or a farmers market works for any baby.
