When to Send Save the Dates

Send save the dates 6 to 8 months before the wedding, 8 to 12 months for a destination wedding, and skip them for engagements of six months or less.

A save the date reaches guests early enough to hold the date and book travel. Most weddings send them 6 to 8 months out.

Timeline

WeddingSend the save the date
Most weddings6 to 8 months before
Local wedding4 to 6 months before
Destination wedding8 to 12 months before
Engagement of six months or lessSkip it and send the invitation

Zola, The Knot, Paperlust and Shutterfly give these ranges. They are rules of thumb.

Send earlier when

  • Guests need to travel by air
  • The wedding falls on a holiday weekend
  • The date is in peak wedding season in your area
  • You have a large guest list from out of town

How to send

  • Email or text a PNG for a fast and low-cost way to reach guests.
  • Mail printed cards if you want a keepsake. See invitation sizes and envelopes.
  • Send it to every guest on the invitation list, including those whose invitation is still undecided.

Common questions

How far in advance should save the dates go out?

Send them 6 to 8 months before the wedding. Destination weddings go out 8 to 12 months before.

Can I send a save the date by email?

Yes. A PNG sent by email or text reaches guests right away and is easy to add to a calendar.
