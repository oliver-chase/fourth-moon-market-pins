When to Send Baby Shower Invitations

Send baby shower invitations 6 to 8 weeks before the shower, or 4 to 6 weeks for a local shower, and ask for replies 10 to 14 days ahead.

A baby shower invitation goes out about 6 to 8 weeks ahead. A small local shower can go out a little later.

Timeline

StepWhen
Send the invitation6 to 8 weeks before, or 4 to 6 weeks for a local shower
Ask for replies by10 to 14 days before
Send a reminderIn the week of the shower
Confirm the final count3 to 5 days before

Evite, Eventifai and Paperless Post give these ranges. Treat them as rules of thumb.

Plan around the parent-to-be

  • Ask for a date that works with the due date and with the parent-to-be's travel.
  • Check with family who need to travel.
  • Send earlier when many guests live out of town.

Sending

Text or email a PNG for a quick reach, and mail a printed card to older relatives. See sizes and envelopes.

Common questions

How far in advance should I send baby shower invitations?

Send them 6 to 8 weeks before the shower. A small local shower can go out 4 to 6 weeks before.

When should guests reply?

Ask for a reply 10 to 14 days before the shower.
